Neutrophil

Look for: 2–5 connected nuclear lobes, dense purple chromatin, pale cytoplasm and fine granules.

Job: Rapid first responder that engulfs and destroys microbes.

Eosinophil

Look for: Bilobed nucleus and conspicuous coarse cytoplasmic granules.

Job: Important in responses to parasites and allergic inflammation.

Lymphocyte

Look for: Dense round nucleus filling most of a relatively small cell, with a narrow blue cytoplasmic rim.

Job: Supports targeted immune responses through B, T and NK lymphocytes.

Monocyte

Look for: Large cell, folded/kidney-shaped nucleus, looser chromatin and abundant gray-blue cytoplasm.

Job: Can enter tissues and differentiate into macrophages or related cells.
