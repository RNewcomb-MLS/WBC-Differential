Before You Start: WBC Identification Key

Look at nuclear shape, chromatin, cytoplasm, granules, and relative cell size. These microscopy-style cells are modeled after Wright-Giemsa appearance.

Neutrophil

3–5 connected nuclear lobes, dark clumped chromatin, pale cytoplasm, fine granules.

First responder: rapidly attacks and engulfs microbes.
Eosinophil

Usually a bilobed nucleus with prominent coarse orange-red granules.

Important in parasite defense and allergic responses.
Lymphocyte

Dense round nucleus takes up most of this smaller cell; thin blue cytoplasmic rim.

Supports targeted immune responses.
Monocyte

Largest cell here; folded kidney/horseshoe nucleus and abundant gray-blue cytoplasm.

Can enter tissues and develop into macrophages.
